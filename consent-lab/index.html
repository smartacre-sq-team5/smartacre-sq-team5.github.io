Consent diagnostics walkthrough

Nothing on this page grants consent for you. Every state below comes from choices you make in the HubSpot banner.

  1. Open DevTools → Network, tick Preserve log, and filter for collect.
  2. Click Open cookie preferences and choose the categories for the state you are testing. Save your choice.
  3. Check that Current preferences shows your choice and that Last update received by tags has changed.
  4. Click Send diagnostic hit. Find the request whose event name is consent_lab_ping and inspect its consent parameters.
  5. Repeat for the next state. Tag Assistant's Consent tab shows the same information.

Tip: the first page view can be sent before your saved preference is applied. Always use the diagnostic hit, sent after the update, as your reading.
