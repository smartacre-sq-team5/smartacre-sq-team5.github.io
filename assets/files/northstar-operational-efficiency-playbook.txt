NORTHSTAR OPERATIONS — THE OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY PLAYBOOK (summary edition)
Fictional training material. Northstar Operations is not a real company.

1. Find the friction
   Run a two-week hand-off audit. For every process step, record who hands work to whom,
   how (email, spreadsheet, ticket, system), and how long the work waits.

2. Size the prize
   Multiply waiting time and rework rates by volume. Convert to hours, then to cost.
   Rank hand-offs by annual cost, not by how loudly people complain about them.

3. Pick the first workflow
   Plot candidates on value versus effort. Choose a high-value, low-effort workflow with
   a single accountable owner and clean source data.

4. Automate with guardrails
   Design the exception path first. Every automated step needs an owner for failures,
   an SLA, and an audit trail.

5. Prove it
   Capture the baseline before go-live. Report weekly against the baseline for 90 days,
   then fold the metric into your standard operations review.
